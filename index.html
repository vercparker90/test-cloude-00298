Pack your shoot kit

Four habits that keep every clip sharp, steady and easy to post.

Hook in 2 seconds

Start on movement or a surprise. The scenery can wait for second three.

Frame for 9:16

Keep action in the center and leave breathing room at the bottom for captions.

Chase soft light

Shoot within an hour of sunrise or sunset. Midday works best near water.

Film local details

Hands, food, signs and sounds make a place feel real. Ask before filming people.
