Eight spots worth the flight

Each location comes with a hook for the first two seconds, the best time to be there and the season to book.

Greece

Santorini

Oia, Cyclades

Slow walk through white alleys ending on the caldera edge, then a whip pan to the sunset.

Light
Golden hour, 45 min before sunset
Season
May–June, September
Japan

Kyoto

Higashiyama and Arashiyama

Open on a lantern-lit lane, cut to the bamboo grove with a rack focus on passing kimonos.

Light
Early morning, before 7:30
Season
Late March–April, November
Indonesia

Bali

Ubud and Uluwatu

Rice terrace drone rise into a cliff temple reveal. Pair it with a calm, ambient sound.

Light
Sunrise on terraces, dusk at the cliffs
Season
April–October
Italy

Amalfi Coast

Positano and Praiano

Lemon-grove balcony pour-over, then a boat shot pulling away from the pastel village.

Light
Mid-morning, soft side light
Season
May–June, September
Iceland

South Coast

Vík and Skógafoss

Black sand wave timelapse, then a walk toward a waterfall with a rainbow in the mist.

Light
Long summer evenings, midnight sun
Season
June–August, aurora in winter
Morocco

Marrakech

Medina and Agafay desert

Souk color burst in handheld, followed by a quiet desert camp at blue hour.

Light
Late afternoon in the medina
Season
March–May, October–November
Canada

Banff

Moraine Lake, Alberta

Canoe on turquoise water with the peaks behind. Start close on the paddle, end wide.

Light
Sunrise, before the crowds
Season
Late June–September
Türkiye

Cappadocia

Göreme

Balloons rising over fairy chimneys. Film from a rooftop, then lift the phone to follow one.

Light
Pre-dawn to sunrise
Season
April–June, September–October

How we plan a shoot

The same four steps on every trip, from the first scouting call to the last export.

  1. Scout the light

    We map sunrise, sunset and crowd peaks for each spot so your best hour is never a guess.

  2. Write the hook

    The first two seconds decide the watch. Every location gets one opening shot that earns the next.

  3. Shoot vertical

    Frame for 9:16 from the start, keep key action in the center and leave room for captions.

  4. Cut and caption

    Tight edits, clear on-screen text and a local detail that makes the clip feel lived in.
